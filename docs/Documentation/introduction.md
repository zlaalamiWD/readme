# About HealthDyne

HealthDyne helps large and small companies alike refine, launch and scale big ideas, fulfilling a mission to empower better healthcare for consumers. HealthDyne was built for speed — helping companies launch their ideas, capitalize on market opportunities, and extend their brand value. Speed creates a competitive advantage and helps you build a  
leadership edge. For those who turn to us for flexible pharmacy solutions, our capabilities accelerate their growth  
and help them move forward with speed and confidence.  
Full API integration capabilities create a seamless consumer experience with your brand. We harness the latest  
connectivity and patient engagement advances and make them available to those who want to scale and grow  
quickly.

HealthDyne operates in partnership with WellDyne – each company operating in different areas of the pharmacy  
industry. WellDyne provides pharmacy benefit management (PBM) services to healthcare payers. And  
HealthDyne provides pharmacy services to all types of healthcare organizations that need to ship medications to  
patients. Because we take a modular approach, HealthDyne clients can choose the exact services – pharmacies,  
technology, consumer engagement, clinical expertise – they need to complement their own capabilities, resources  
and talent in order to grow their business.
